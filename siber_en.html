Our 4 Pillars of Business Solutions

Clients buy security for 2 reasons: Fear (Risk) or Obligation (Compliance). Understand the categories below to offer targeted solutions.

Finding the Gaps

Audits & Offensive Security

Basic Concept: We act as ethical hackers (White Hats). Our job is to breach the client's system before the bad guys do, then tell them how to fix it.

Service Catalog (What are we selling?):

  • Pentest (Penetration Testing): Manual simulated attacks on the client's web, mobile apps, or network.
  • Vulnerability Scanning: Automated scanning using software to find common flaws. Cheaper than a Pentest.
  • Architecture/Configuration Audit: Checking the "blueprint" of the client's servers and cloud to ensure they are set up correctly.

Sales Angle (Pitching)

  • Target Client: Companies releasing new applications or those mandated by regulators.
  • Pain Point: "We don't know if our application is safe from hackers."
  • How to Sell: Offer it as a "Health Check-up". This is often a door-opener to sell advanced services (Defense/SOC) after we show them the many flaws in their system.
Active 24/7 Protection

Cyber Operations (Cyberdefense)

Basic Concept: Building defensive walls and standing guard day and night. If a hacker tries to get in, our team will detect the alarm and block them.

Service Catalog (What are we selling?):

  • SOC (Security Operations Center): 24/7 network monitoring service for the client.
  • MSSP (Managed Security Service Provider): We fully operate the client's advanced Antivirus (EDR) and Firewalls.
  • Incident Response (CSIRT): Cyber firefighters. Called only when the client has already been hacked (e.g., Ransomware).

Sales Angle (Pitching)

  • Target Client: Medium-to-large enterprises, hospitals, banks, manufacturing.
  • Pain Point: "It's hard and expensive to find IT Security talent to work the night shift."
  • How to Sell: Sell "Peace of Mind". This is a long-term subscription (Recurring Revenue). Show that outsourcing (MSSP) is much cheaper than building an internal SOC team.
Bureaucracy & Rules

Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC)

Basic Concept: GRC is about paperwork, law, and strategy. It doesn't involve technical hands-on work (like coding), but rather sets the company's policies.

Service Catalog (What are we selling?):

  • Compliance Consulting (ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, GDPR): Helping clients get certified or comply with data privacy laws.
  • vCISO (Virtual Chief Information Security Officer): Renting out a Director-level expert part-time to guide the client's IT strategy.
  • Risk Assessment: Calculating potential losses if client data leaks to determine the appropriate security budget.

Sales Angle (Pitching)

  • Target Client: C-Level (CEO, CFO), pre-IPO companies, E-commerce, Fintech.
  • Pain Point: "Our biggest client is threatening to cancel our contract if we don't have an ISO 27001 certificate."
  • How to Sell: This is a "Mandatory" service due to state regulations or tender requirements. Sell the fact that compliance = winning more business for them.
The Human Factor

Training & Awareness

Basic Concept: 80% of hacks happen because naive employees click on malicious links. This service trains client staff so they aren't easily fooled by hacker tricks.

Service Catalog (What are we selling?):

  • Phishing Simulations: We send fake emails (like fake lottery wins or HR warnings) to see which employees click them.
  • E-Learning & Training Sessions: Interactive learning modules (videos/quizzes) on creating strong passwords and safe remote work practices.

Sales Angle (Pitching)

  • Target Client: HR teams or IT Managers in companies of any size.
  • Pain Point: "Our IT systems are advanced, but employees keep falling for online scams."
  • How to Sell: This service is very easy to demonstrate. Offer a free trial (send 50 phishing simulation emails) and show the results. They will be shocked at how many fell for it, and then buy our training services.
